About the company

Family owned in New Bedford

A home is personal.
So is the work we put into it.

Carlos Vinyl Siding is a Portuguese family-owned home improvement company based in New Bedford, Massachusetts. We help South Coast homeowners care for and renew their homes with vinyl siding, replacement windows, roofing, aluminum trim, gutters and related exterior improvements.

From a single update to a full exterior transformation, the conversation starts with your home, your priorities and the details that matter to you.
